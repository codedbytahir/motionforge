# Accessible Delivery

## Inputs
Destination, captions, key information, motion preferences and player interaction.

## Procedure
1. Keep essential meaning available in held readable frames and text alternatives.
2. Provide keyboard controls and visible focus; avoid autoplay with sound.
3. Offer reduced-motion preview without changing the exact offline reference unexpectedly.
4. Check flashing, safe areas, contrast, captions and text legibility in the actual delivered player.

## Acceptance gate
Keyboard and reduced-motion checks, readable copy and caption timing.

## Avoid
An accessibility checklist is not certification; report tested behavior and limitations.
