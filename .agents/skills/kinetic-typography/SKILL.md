# Kinetic Typography

## Inputs
Exact copy, font files/license, safe area, output size and smallest playback size.

## Procedure
1. Load fonts before measurement or capture; measure actual strings and reserve stable layout bounds.
2. Use deliberate weights and contrast; inspect at 720p and phone-width presentation.
3. Stagger words or lines only when hierarchy benefits; derive progress from frame and entrance start.
4. Hold readable text after entrances; test longest strings, clipping and multilingual shaping.

## Acceptance gate
Font readiness, no overflow, readable held frame and compression preview.

## Avoid
Never sacrifice clarity for continuous movement or assume hidden glyphs produce a natural typing caret.
