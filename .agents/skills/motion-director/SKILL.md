# Motion Director

## Inputs
Audience, intended action, runtime, aspect ratio, brand constraints and supported delivery formats.

## Procedure
1. Write a one-sentence promise and factual claim list.
2. Build a beat sheet with start frame, end frame, message and proof asset for each shot.
3. Choose only the specialist skills needed; route vectors to Trolic and film effects to MotionForge.
4. Define a validation gate and deliverable for each specialist. Separate implementation, visual review and publishing permissions.

## Acceptance gate
Brief plus shot manifest; every claim backed by product evidence.

## Avoid
More effects do not imply better storytelling. Never invent product capabilities.
